Women’s Health and Digital Empowerment Initiative

Organization: Janñah Striver Support Initiative
Location: Kogi State, Nigeria
Project stage: Early-stage proposal

1. About Our Organization

Janñah Striver Support Initiative is a grassroots nonprofit initiative committed to supporting widows, vulnerable women, single mothers, and elderly women. Our work focuses on women’s empowerment, vocational skills development, and helping elderly women access medical support.

2. The Problem We Want to Address

Women and girls in underserved communities may face barriers to healthcare, reliable health information, digital education, and economic opportunities. Limited resources can make it difficult for them to access healthcare services or develop skills that could improve their financial independence.

3. Our Proposed Solution

We aim to develop a community-based initiative combining women’s health support and digital empowerment. Our proposed activities include:

* Sharing reliable general health information and guidance on finding healthcare services.
* Helping women identify appropriate healthcare providers and referral options.
* Connecting women and girls with digital skills learning opportunities in basic coding, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.
* Seeking partnerships with healthcare professionals, technology trainers, and community organizations.
* Using digital platforms to share information about available opportunities and mobilize support.

4. Who We Aim to Support

Our intended beneficiaries include women and girls in underserved communities in Kogi State, particularly widows, single mothers, vulnerable women, and elderly women.

5. Current Progress

We have an organizational website and social media presence to communicate our mission and seek support. However, this proposed digital initiative is still at an early stage. We have not yet developed a dedicated software platform or completed a formal pilot.

6. Planned Implementation

1. Engage community members to understand their needs and priorities.
2. Identify potential healthcare and digital training partners.
3. Develop a simple digital prototype to share useful information and opportunities.
4. Plan a small pilot based on available resources.
5. Collect participant feedback and monitor progress.
6. Improve the initiative based on lessons learned.

7. Resources Needed

We are seeking grant funding, technical guidance, suitable digital equipment, internet access, healthcare partnerships, training resources, and transportation support to help implement and evaluate the proposed initiative.

8. Long-Term Vision

Our vision is to help women and girls overcome barriers to healthcare information, digital education, and economic opportunity, while building a sustainable community-based model that can reach more underserved women over time.
